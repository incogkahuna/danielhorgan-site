Method

Shot evaluation. Note format.

  1. Observation

    What happened, in plain visual language.

  2. Where

    Timestamp or frame range.

  3. Dimension

    Motion, timing, anatomy, physics, temporal consistency, lighting, VFX integration or prompt adherence.

  4. Severity

    Blocking, major or minor.

  5. Fix or verdict

    The change required, or pass/fail with one reason.

Example. Bus explosion, first pass, 0:04–0:11. Observation: crowd holds static poses while the fire spreads. Dimension: motion, prompt adherence. Severity: blocking. Fix: make crowd motion the priority in the prompt; pin a last frame in which every figure has moved.
