Method

How I note a shot. Specific enough that someone else can fix it.

  1. Observation

    What happened, in plain visual language.

  2. Where

    Timestamp or frame range.

  3. Dimension

    Motion, timing, anatomy, physics, transition, VFX integration or prompt adherence.

  4. Severity

    Blocking, which breaks the shot, or minor, which is noticeable but usable.

  5. Fix or verdict

    What needs to change, or pass/fail with one reason.
